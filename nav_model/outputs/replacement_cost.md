# Replacement cost + FCF

Value/share = [depreciated replacement cost of the fleet - net debt & claims + PV (9%) of equity FCF generated over the replacement lead time] / shares. Replacement cost uses 2026 new-build $/kW and remaining-life fractions only - no earnings or power prices. FCF (after overhead, interest, tax, preferred; includes retail cash) is the only scenario-dependent part.
