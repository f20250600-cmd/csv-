### NAV + FCF generated (forward-NAV method, no double counting)

Equity FCF 2027-2030 (after overhead, interest, 20% cash tax and preferred dividends; includes retail platform cash; no growth capex, no buybacks) + equity NAV at end-2030 of the remaining asset life. Implied return = (value at end-2030 / price)^(1/4.25) - 1.
